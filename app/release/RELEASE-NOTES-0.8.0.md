## Takkar 0.8.0 (beta)

Install over your current Takkar: Android shows **Update**, and your games and streak stay.

**New: reports that tell you what to do next** (for live tests in your tutor's league)
- Your test report now shows how the test went: accuracy on the answers you gave, marks lost to wrong answers, your pace against the time allowed, and questions you never opened.
- **What to do next:** up to three pieces of advice from your own answers, the ones worth most marks first. For example: you rushed the questions you got wrong, you left questions unopened, or a topic to practise, with a button to practise it.
- **My progress:** your scores across your league's tests, and each topic marked strong, getting there or needs work. A topic is judged only after four questions on it, so the advice gets sharper with every test.
- Advice comes in Hindi if you chose Hindi questions.
- It is worked out by fixed rules from your live test answers. No AI, and nothing from your own duels or practice.

**For tutors**
- Tap any student in a class report to see their progress across tests, their report for each test, and the advice they are shown.
