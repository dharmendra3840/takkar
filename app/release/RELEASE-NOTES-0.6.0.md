## Takkar 0.6.0 (beta)

Install over your current Takkar: Android shows **Update**, and your games and streak stay.

**New**
- Seven exams: SSC CGL, CHSL and GD, IBPS PO and RRB, RRB NTPC and Group D. Duels, practice and mocks follow each exam's marking and timing.
- Questions in Hindi: every question type, with a हिंदी / EN switch in duels, practice and mocks. Challenge messages can be shared in Hindi.
- Duel a friend, live: share a code and play the same ten questions at the same moment, with their score as you play. Free, with Google sign-in.
- Tutor leagues: join your tutor's league with their link or code, using your real name (only your tutor sees it).
- Live tests (beta): sit your tutor's timed tests in your exam's pattern. Your answers save as you go, and you see your rank and topic report when your tutor releases results.
- User IDs: signed-in players pick a unique user ID; nicknames no longer need to be unique.
