## Takkar 0.10.0 (beta)

Install over your current Takkar: Android shows **Update**, and your games and streak stay.

**New: bank-exam questions**
- **Seating arrangement:** people in a row or around a table, with clues to work out who sits where.
- **Puzzles:** people on the floors of a building.
- **Data interpretation:** tables of figures (sales, admissions, a budget shared by percentage) with questions on totals, averages, percentages and ratios.
- They come up most in IBPS PO and IBPS RRB, and sometimes in railway and SSC duels. Practise each from the Practice tab, and tutors can put them in live tests.
- Clues show as a numbered list and figures as a table, in Hindi or English. These questions get **twice the usual time** in a duel, as they take longer to read.
- Every puzzle is checked to have exactly one answer.

Challenge codes from older versions still open with their own questions. To play a challenge or squad duel with the new questions, update Takkar.
