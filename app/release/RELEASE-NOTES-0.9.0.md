## Takkar 0.9.0 (beta)

Install over your current Takkar: Android shows **Update**, and your games and streak stay.

**For tutors: class insights**
- **Re-teach first:** each class report lists the topics most of your batch got wrong, the ones worth most marks in your exam first.
- **Common wrong answers:** questions where many students picked the same wrong option, with that option and the right answer, so you can see the misunderstanding.
- **Class insights across your tests** (from your league page): the batch's average test by test, topics to re-teach across all tests, students who are slipping or have missed tests, and the most improved. Tap a student for their progress and the advice they're shown.

**Fixes**
- Tutors: **Download CSV** and the paper's **Download** now work in the Android app. They open the share sheet with the file attached, so you can send it or save it. The CSV opens in Excel with Hindi names intact.
