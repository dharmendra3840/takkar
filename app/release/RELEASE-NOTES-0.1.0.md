First public beta of Takkar for Android.

- Daily duel: ten Quant and Reasoning questions, 30 seconds each, exam marking (+4 / −1), a rival and step-by-step solutions
- Friend challenges with a shareable code
- Squads, ranks and an educator league demo
- Your games are saved only on your phone; the app sends nothing to us

For aspirants aged 18 and over. Android 6.0 or newer.
