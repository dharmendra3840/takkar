## Takkar 0.7.0 (beta)

Install over your current Takkar: Android shows **Update**, and your games and streak stay.

**New: reminders**
- Daily reminder: pick a time between 7 am and 9 pm (You → Daily reminder, or when you set your daily goal). It's skipped on days you've already met your goal, and it tells you when your streak is about to end. Never between 10 pm and 7 am.
- Live test reminders: 10 minutes before your tutor's live test, and when it opens. Tap **Remind me** on the test, or turn them on under You.
- Reminders come in Hindi if you chose Hindi questions. Tapping one opens Takkar where you need to be.
- Reminders are set on your phone: nothing extra is sent to Takkar. Android asks once whether Takkar may show notifications.

**Fixes**
- Tutors: the schedule form no longer misses a tap after you change the date, time or duration.
