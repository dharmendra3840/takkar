Takkar: design board

Key screens for the exam-first app and rooms. Mid-fidelity: real colours, type and copy, sample numbers. Read with design/README.md. Phone frames are 300 px wide; the desktop frame shows the educator dashboard.

1. Onboarding: exam first

Three quick choices before anything else, then one screen that explains the marking for their exam, then the first duel. Sign-in stays where it is (Google, phone, guest) and keeps the exam.

2. Home, practice and the mini mock

Home answers "what should I do now?" for their exam. Practice replaces the League tab: topic practice (unlimited) and mini mocks with real exam timing.

3. Rooms: the student side

Rooms replace the League and Squad demos. A student joins from a link their teacher shares, takes the class test at the set time, and sees their rank when the test closes.

4. Rooms: the educator side

Educators apply once, then create rooms in a six-step wizard, add their own questions (one by one or a whole sheet), and run tests from a dashboard that works best on a computer.

Sample numbers and names are illustrative. Exam patterns must be verified against the latest official notices before launch (see 01-exam-patterns.md).
