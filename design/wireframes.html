Takkar: design board

Exam-first app with four clear ideas: Duel (one-to-one), Squad (study group), League (an educator's class) and Room (a live test at a set time). Mid-fidelity: real colours, type and copy; sample numbers. Read with design/README.md.

1. Onboarding: exam first

The exam comes before anything else, so the app is about their exam from the first tap. Then sign-in (Google, phone or guest), nickname and 18+, and one screen with the exam's rules before the first duel.

2. Home and Practice

Home answers "what should I do now?". Practice adds topic practice and mini mocks that feel like the real computer-based test.

3. Duel: one-to-one

Against the rival bot (as now), or one friend, live. If the friend does not come within 10 minutes, it becomes a challenge they can play within 24 hours.

4. Squad: study group

Anyone creates one (students or a teacher), up to 20. One squad duel a day, a table, a weekly mission and reactions. Leave, remove and report are real actions.

5. League and live Room: the student side

A League is an approved educator's class (up to 100). The educator schedules Rooms: live tests at a set time. The lobby opens 15 minutes before; at the start the doors close, and late students cannot join.

6. The educator side

Approved educators create leagues and rooms, add their own text questions (one by one or a sheet), and run everything from a dashboard that works best on a computer.

7. Website: "I'm preparing for…"

The hero asks the exam first and starts onboarding with it selected.

Sample numbers and names are illustrative. Exam patterns must be verified against the latest official notices (see 01-exam-patterns.md).
