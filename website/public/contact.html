Grievance contact

If you have a complaint about Takkar, its content or how we handle your data, message us on Instagram with the word Grievance, or send a report in the app and choose Something else. During the beta, complaints are handled by Takkar’s founder.

  • We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.
  • A named grievance officer and email address will be listed here before Takkar opens to everyone.

Found a security problem?

Please message us on Instagram that you have found one, without the details, and we will tell you how to send them privately. Do not share them publicly until we have fixed it. Thank you for helping keep players safe.
