Questions tutors ask

Do my students have to pay?

No. Students never pay to be in your league or to sit your tests.

Do I need my own app or website?

No. Your students use Takkar in any browser at playtakkar.in/play, or the Android app. You only share your league’s code or link.

Can I use my own questions?

Not yet. Tests use Takkar’s question bank in the patterns of SSC CGL, CHSL and GD, IBPS PO and RRB, and RRB NTPC and Group D, with every answer calculated and checked. Your own questions may come later.

Who sees my students’ information?

Only you, as their tutor: their real name, nickname, user ID and results in your league. Other students see neither. You agree to use it only to teach them; see the educator terms.

How will you stop copying in live tests?

Each student gets the questions and options in a different order, the Android app blocks screenshots during the test, and your report shows how often each student left the app. Students don’t see scores or answers until you release results.

Can school students use it?

No. Takkar is for people aged 18 and over, so please invite only adult students.

Is there any prize money or contest?

No. Takkar has no cash prizes, betting or paid contests, and tutors may not run any through it.

Takkar is in beta. Independent and not affiliated with any exam-conducting organisation.
