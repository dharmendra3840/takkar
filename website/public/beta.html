Takkar for Android

No download needed: play in your browser on any phone or computer. The browser version is always the newest.

Pick your exam (SSC CGL, IBPS PO or RRB NTPC) and every duel follows its marking and timing: ten quant and reasoning questions, a rival, and step-by-step solutions. Practise any topic, take a 25-question mini mock, challenge a friend with a code, and preview squads and an educator’s league (with sample players).

This is a beta for aspirants aged 18 and over. Sign in with Google or your phone number to keep your progress on any device, or play as a guest: then your games stay only on your phone. Things may change or reset between versions.

Download for Android

About 7.0 MB · Android 6.0 or newer · Free

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This is normal for apps that are not on the Play Store yet.
  3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Takkar and play your first duel.

On an iPhone?

Play in Safari. To keep Takkar on your home screen, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen, like an app.

Check the download

The file comes from Takkar’s public GitHub page, takkar-app releases, where every version and its changes are listed. GitHub handles the download, so it sees your IP address, as any website does.
