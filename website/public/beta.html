Takkar for Android

No download needed: play in your browser on any phone or computer. The Android app is the same game, for people who prefer an app.

Play today’s duel right now: ten quant and reasoning questions, exam marking, a rival, and step-by-step solutions. Challenge a friend with a code, try squads and see how an educator’s league works.

This is an early prototype for aspirants aged 18 and over. You play as a guest: your games are saved only on your phone. The app sends us nothing about you, only a report or flagged question if you choose to send one. Google and phone sign-in are coming soon. Things may change or reset between versions.

Download for Android

About 6.8 MB · Android 6.0 or newer · Free

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This is normal for apps that are not on the Play Store yet.
  3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Takkar and play your first duel.

On an iPhone?

Play in Safari. To keep Takkar on your home screen, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen, like an app.

Check the download

The file comes from Takkar’s public GitHub page, takkar-app releases, where every version and its changes are listed. GitHub handles the download, so it sees your IP address, as any website does.
