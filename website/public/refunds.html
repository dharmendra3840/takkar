Refunds and cancellation

Takkar Plus

Takkar Plus is not on sale yet, so nobody can be charged today. The full refund policy will be published here before we take any payment.

What we have already decided

  • Plus will cost ₹99 a month (introductory price), paid by UPI Autopay or card through Razorpay. Takkar never sees your card or UPI details.
  • Before you pay, you will see the price, how often it renews and how to cancel. No fake timers and no hidden costs.
  • You can cancel in two taps from your Profile. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • We email you 3 days before every renewal, and send a receipt for every payment.
  • Cancelling in your UPI app or with your bank also ends Plus at the end of the paid period.
  • Deleting your account cancels Plus first, so you are never charged again.

Questions

Write to support@takkar.info. We reply to payment questions within 24 hours.
