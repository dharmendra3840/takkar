That is all. We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts or location. This website sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Who sees it

Only the Takkar team. The site is hosted by Vercel and the list is stored with Upstash, our database provider; both process it on our behalf. We never sell or share your data.

Like any website, our host handles basic request data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block attacks. To stop spam sign-ups we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from your IP address for one hour, then it is deleted. We do not use it to identify you.

Your choices

  • Leave the list at any time by replying to our email or writing to privacy@takkar.info.
  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at the same address.
  • If you are under 18, please do not join. If we learn an under-18 has joined, we delete their email.

Questions or complaints

Write to privacy@takkar.info. We acknowledge every message within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 15 days. See the grievance contact for more.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Takkar is an early prototype. Independent and not affiliated with any exam-conducting organisation.
