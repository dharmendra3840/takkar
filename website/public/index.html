Questions people ask

Can I win money?

No. Takkar has no cash prizes, betting or paid contests. You play for rank, streaks and practice.

Who can play?

Takkar is for people who are 18 or older.

Where do the questions come from?

Our own engine generates them and calculates every answer. Nothing is copied from books or test series.

Do I need an account?

No. Everyone plays as a guest for now: your nickname, games and streak stay in your browser or on your phone. Google and phone sign-in, to keep your streak on any device, are coming soon. We never ask for your contacts.

What can I do in the beta?

Today: duels in your exam’s marking and timing against a rival, friend challenges with a code, topic practice, one mini mock a day and the steps for every mistake. Coming next: sign-in to keep your progress on any device, squads, educator leagues, and English and General Awareness.

Which parts of the exam are covered?

Quant (Maths) and Reasoning, in each exam’s marking and timing. English and General Awareness are not in Takkar yet. Questions are in English.

Where is my progress saved?

In this beta, in your browser or on your phone only. Clearing the site’s or the app’s data removes it. You can download a copy any time from You → Download my data.

Is Takkar connected to any exam body?

No. Takkar is independent and not affiliated with any organisation that conducts exams.

When can I play?

Now. Takkar is in beta: play free in your browser on any phone or computer, or get the Android app. The app is on Takkar’s public GitHub page, and the beta page shows its fingerprint so you can check the file.
