About Takkar

Takkar, also called playtakkar after its address playtakkar.in · In beta since October 2026 · Updated 4 November 2026

Takkar is a free daily quiz duel for people in India preparing for government exams. Pick your exam and every duel follows its pattern: ten quant and reasoning questions with that exam’s marking and time a question, against a rival, with the steps for every answer. Takkar (टक्कर) means a head-on contest in Hindi.

Who it’s for

Aspirants aged 18 and over preparing for SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC GD, IBPS PO, IBPS RRB PO and Clerk, RRB NTPC and RRB Group D, and the tutors and coaching centres who teach them. Each exam has a guide to its pattern, also in Hindi.

What you can do

  • Play a daily duel: ten questions in your exam’s pattern against a rival, or a friend’s challenge code.
  • Practise one topic at a time, with the quick method and solved questions, and a new question every day.
  • Take a 25-question mini mock with your exam’s timing.
  • Study with friends in a squad, or join your tutor’s league.

No money, ever

Takkar is not a betting, gaming-for-money or earning app. There are no cash prizes, wallets, paid contests or money rewards, and you cannot earn or lose money on it. Playing is free; a paid plan for extra practice is planned for later, and nobody is charged in the beta.

Independent

Takkar is not affiliated with SSC, IBPS, the Railway Recruitment Boards or any other exam body. Every question is made by Takkar’s question engine with the answer worked out, not copied. Patterns are checked against the latest reported notices, and the official notice always wins.

Where to play

In any browser at playtakkar.in/play, or in the Android app. Questions or problems: see Contact.
