Educator terms

Version 2026-10-beta · Last updated 3 November 2026 · For tutors and coaching centres verified on the Takkar beta

Draft. These terms will be reviewed by a lawyer before paid educator plans start. They add to Takkar’s terms of use, which still apply.

Becoming a verified educator

  • You are 18 or over and sign in with a Google account.
  • You run the channel or coaching centre you name, and you prove it: with a code on your channel or website, on a video call, or by meeting the Takkar team. We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN or other ID documents.
  • Apply only for your own channel or centre, or with written permission from the person who runs it.

Your students’ information

  • In your leagues you see each student’s real name, nickname, user ID, scores and test reports, and how often they left the app during a test. You never see their email address or phone number.
  • Use this only to teach and guide your students.
  • Do not sell it, share it outside your centre, publish rankings with real names without your students’ agreement, or use it to contact or advertise to students outside Takkar.
  • When a student leaves your league, stop using their results for anything new.

Tests and fair play

  • No cash prizes, betting, paid contests or rewards worth money may be linked to Takkar tests.
  • Do not promise selection, ranks or results in any exam.
  • Takkar’s questions belong to Takkar. You may share a test paper with your own students; do not republish or sell it.
  • Takkar is for people aged 18 and over. Do not invite younger students.

Plans and payments

  • The beta educator plan is free. Paid plans are coming soon: the price will be shown before you pay, and nothing is charged without your agreement.

If something goes wrong

  • If you break these terms, or students report abuse, we may warn you, pause or close your leagues and tests, remove your verified badge, or suspend or remove your account. You can ask us to review a decision through our contact page.
  • You can stop being an educator at any time. If you delete your account, your leagues close and your students see a notice.

Contact

Questions about these terms: see our contact page.

Takkar is in beta. Independent and not affiliated with any exam-conducting organisation.
